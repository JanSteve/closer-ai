1. The Problem: A Tuesday Night on OMR, Chennai

The Hair-On-Fire Scenario: At 8:15 PM, a buyer clicks a Facebook ad for an apartment on OMR, Chennai. The lead sits untouched overnight because human telecallers leave at 6:00 PM. By 11:45 AM next morning when someone finally calls, the lead is in an office meeting or forgot clicking. ₹850 in ad spend is incinerated.
  • Fatal Response Latency: Leads called within 5 minutes convert at 21x higher rates. In India, average human telecaller callback time is 4.2 hours.
  • Severe Call Center Turnover: BPOs and sales desks in Chennai/Bangalore experience 45% to 65% annual employee attrition.
  • Language Disconnect: Generic English alienates vernacular buyers. Customers in Tamil Nadu buy when spoken to in respectful, natural Chennai Tanglish.

2. The Solution: Turnkey "AI Employees" (Not Software)

Inspired by autonomous operator platforms like Foundable's "Ted", CLOSER AI sells turnkey digital employees who start closing deals on Day 1:

  • Priya (Tamil Nadu): Fluent Chennai Tanglish closer trained on RERA, carpet areas, and site visits.
  • Ritu (North India): Warm, consultative Hindi/Hinglish customer representative.
  • Sophia (Global): Articulate, poised English closer for luxury assets and NRIs.
  • Omnichannel Auto-Sync: Calls within 30 seconds, and immediately drops brochures and map pins onto the buyer's WhatsApp.
