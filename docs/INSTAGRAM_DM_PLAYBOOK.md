## 🎯 The Psychology of Selling to Luxury Realtors
Top luxury real estate agents in Beverly Hills, Miami, Austin, and New York have three distinct traits:
1. **Time-Starved Ego:** They love prestige, concierge service, and their personal brand. They hate boring tech jargon, long emails, and feeling like an amateur.
2. **Burned by Cheap Leads:** They spend $5,000–$15,000/month running Instagram and Zillow ads, but complain that 90% of leads are "tire kickers" or "bad numbers".
3. **Speed Obsession:** They know deep down that when a high-net-worth buyer inquires on a $5M listing at 8:30 PM on a Sunday, they cannot call them immediately. If someone calls first, they steal the client.
